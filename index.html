Exact-Geometry MPC-CBF for Autonomous Boat Docking in Narrow Slips

A real-time NMPC whose safety constraints are discrete-time control barrier functions on the exact signed distance between the polygonal hull and the berth. Enclosing circles, ellipses, and disc covers shrink, and can empty, the orientation-dependent feasible docking space; the exact polygon preserves it.

Orientation-dependent feasible docking space

For a footprint symmetric about the body axis, a pose with clearance dsafe inside the slip must satisfy |y| ≤ ½(Wslip − wF(θ)) − dsafe, a corridor in the lateral offset and the heading. The curves below are the boundary of the admissible vessel-centre region inside a 1.10 m slip at a fixed heading offset, evaluated with the smooth clearance of each footprint. The enclosing circle does not merely shrink this region: for a slender hull it empties it. Drag the hull to vary θ.

Docking behaviour on representative scenarios

Five fixed scenarios isolate the closed-loop behaviour before the randomized sweep: a fairway turn-in at rgap = 1.0 (S1), two aligned approaches at rgap = 1.0 and 0.5 (S2, S3), a near-bound aligned entry at rgap = 0.25 (S4), and a hard entry from the sweep at rgap = 0.35 (S5). Every controller is shown from the same initial condition.

Narrow-slip sweep

The slip width is reduced from 2.475 m (rgap = 3.5) to 0.688 m (rgap = 0.25) over ten values, with the same 30 randomized entries at each width (1800 closed-loop runs). All controllers use a uniform 10 s horizon. Success of each footprint collapses at its geometric bound Wmin(F): the proposed controller docks 30/30 at 0.743 m and 23/30 at 0.688 m with no collisions; the pointwise polygon-DC form of the same clearance records two collisions.
